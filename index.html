HEALTH ANDFITNESS TIPS

Simple habits for a healthier lifestyle.

1. Balanced Diet

Eat vegetables, fruits, whole grains, protein-rich foods, and healthy fats.

2. Stay Hydrated

Drink water regularly throughout the day, especially before and after exercise.

3. Get Enough Sleep

Maintain a regular sleep schedule to support recovery and overall well-being.

4. Exercise Regularly

Stay active consistently and gradually increase your exercise intensity.

5. Warm Up

Prepare your body with light movement and dynamic stretches before intense workouts.

6. Take Rest Days

Allow your body time to recover between demanding workouts.
